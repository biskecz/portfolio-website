SMA Backtester

SMA Backtester is a Python-based backtesting framework for testing a moving-average crossover strategy on historical AAPL data. It supports configurable SMA periods, transaction costs, performance and risk metrics, trade analysis, and visualization. The strategy is compared against a passive Buy & Hold approach to evaluate its performance. This project was built as a learning exercise in quantitative finance, backtesting, and modular Python development.

The project is designed as a modular workflow for exploring market behavior and developing practical quantitative finance skills.
